# assignment-of-information-managment

Past

In the past, computers were very large, expensive, and slow. They were mainly used for basic calculations and scientific or military purposes. Early computers used vacuum tubes and occupied entire rooms. People had to use punched cards or other complicated methods to give instructions to computers.

Present

Today, computers are smaller, faster, and more powerful. We use computers for education, communication, business, entertainment, banking, designing, and many other activities. Laptops, desktops, tablets, and smartphones allow people to access information and communicate with others from almost anywhere.

Future

In the future, computers are expected to become even faster, smarter, and more efficient. Artificial intelligence, robotics, and advanced technologies will allow computers to perform more complex tasks and work more like humans. Computers may become smaller and more integrated into everyday objects, making our lives easier and more connected.
